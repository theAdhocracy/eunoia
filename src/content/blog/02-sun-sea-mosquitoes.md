Everything needs a beginning. For us, the beginning was quite eventful ‒ just not in a good way. We had queues; we had snow; we had delays and in the end our beginning had to wait 24 hours before it could actually call itself a start. Our beginning was being tired and hungry and quite regularly confused. Our beginning had nice officials and form nazis in equal measure; it had donuts at 4am and pizza at six. It had a distinct lack of pens.

Yet ultimately it was a beginning. We had begun. So now, as we progress through our infancy just a few days after said beginning, it all seems rather worthwhile. 48 hours in the snow, cold and JFK airport is completely justified for one day (let alone six weeks) in St. Maarten with sun and sea and $1.50 Desperados on the beach.

Who knows, maybe even the rampant mosquitoes will soon be forgiven…
