Thus we enter the first weekend with J&S; so far so good. We began by heading into Phillipsburg with Sean to pick up some sods (for his garden) and managed to continue progress with the technological revamp of the Kennelly household by ways of a new mobile phone and new camera.

Lunch surrounded by multi-million pound mega-yachts was pretty novel (and pretty delicious – only the best burgers can be consumed by the world's richest deck-hands) and so we decided to do something touristy and check out the airport.

It doesn’t sound like a normal tourist destination but the airport on St. Maarten is probably the best in the world for spectators; aeroplanes land right over head as you lounge on a golden beach and take off about fifty-metres-and-a-fence away. These aren’t island hoppers either; we’re talking medium sized airbuses and 757/777’s (or so I’m informed). It was surprisingly exhilarating watching these huge planes flying straight towards you from over the Carribbean sea. It was also pretty novel to effectively have your sunglasses stolen by a Boeing 757! Suffice to say we tried to stand in the full blast of engines during take-off and ultimately failed. My shades are somewhere in the ocean, hopefully headed for a nice tropical island to call their new home. Oh well… at least I didn’t fall over! (\*cough\*)
