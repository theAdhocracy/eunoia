Welcome to the Eunoia Studio Blog. For those new here (which is everyone at this point) the blog serves two separate purposes. First and foremost it is a place where Murray and Mike can post articles and photographs about their lives and travels. However, looking to the ever-distant future, this blog is also designed to be the official company blog for Eunoia Studios.

As such every now and then simple reports and updates will be posted here regarding the company, the service and anything related with the two. Hopefully this won’t be too great an inconvenience for those browsing purely for the latest details of the founders lives; please just realise that these company-related blogs are far more for our own interest, to record growth and help with future decisions.

On to the recent Eunoia news then. As many may have noticed the blog has undergone some serious changes. Although these are by no means complete and still most of the main features have yet to be turned online the backbone of the layout and graphical design for Eunoia Theme v.1 has now been finalised and released.

We’d love to hear feedback, both on what we have so far and on future developments to the theme. Any suggestions as to content or features, any issues or bugs, please don’t hesitate to comment below - even if you just want to give praise! Seriously, we can take it 😉
Once again, apologies that this bares no relevance to the current adventures being had, hope no one minds too greatly. As a quick note along those lines we have also made sure that the author of the article is displayed on the front page, on the article itself and on the RSS feed. If the author is Eunoia Studios you can be sure it will be a company related post.
