So after many hours of reading a text book on diving and watching dvds, given to us by the dive centre, we were ready to take our first written tests and a final exam which we both passed with flying colours. We were not ready to go for our first open water dive in the afternoon but first we headed for lunch at Abu Gazie where we had the most amazing shawame which was stuff full of meat for only $6! After this we headed to the gear store and got kitted out with stuff and put it all together. We then headed out on a boat to the diving area, the journey took a while as we lost an engine and the dive site was at the other end of the island. Once we arrived we were briefed on what we were gonna do once at the bottom (which was around 7-8 meters). Then we got our kit on made some checks and once we had inflated our BCDs we then got into the water, where we waited on a line for everyone else to get ready. After this we started to ascend down one by one but Murray went first and could not equalize one of his ears so had to come back to the surface after a few meters. I managed to equalize successfully and so reached the bottom where I carried out some skills like taking the regulator out of my mouth and putting it back in and clearing it. I also had to drop it at my side and recover it as well as letting water into my mask and clearing it. Once I had done these skills the instructor took us for a swim close to the reef there where I saw loads of fish and a moray eel. I was under water for about 30mins before coming backup. After this we set off back to the dive centre.

It was great under water although the visibility could have been better. It was a shame that Murray didn’t make it down as the reef was quite good. The scuba instructor thought he might have congestion which takes a while to get rid of before he could go diving again.
