As some of you know already, Mike was scheduled to have his final scuba dive this morning. However when we turned up the dive-operator recommended he waited so that I could catch up as it was easier diving with a buddy (that’s what they call a dive partner, not a retro speech reversion).

Anyway we decided that, as we were wearing swim shorts, we’d go swimming at a nearby beach. The resort there had built a dive platform out in the bay which we swam out to; we’d been there a few minutes when Mike suddenly yells “What on earth is that?!”

Looking over the side of this small platform, directly below us in the water was a very large fish. First thoughts: reef shark. On closer inspection though it turned out to lack the standard fins (although did have a large dorsal fin) so we believe it to have been a barracuda. I’m not sure which would have been worse…

Nevertheless we stayed on that dive platform for quite some time, hoping it would go away (we could just see it by peering over the edge - it really was big, about a metre/metre and a half in length) before eventually taking a running leap and striking out to shore as quickly as possible. I doubt it was harmful in any way but try telling your brain that when your eyes are registering a huge, streamlined fish with a mouth of razor teeth and the message soon gets lost in translation.

Regardless by the time we reached shore I noticed quite a few other swimmers watching us incredulously. They obviously hadn’t looked _under_ the water yet.
