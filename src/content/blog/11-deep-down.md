It’s been a few days since my last blog and I’m now gonna take you back a few days and tell you about my second dive and pool exercises and Murray’s attempt at a second dive and pool exercises.

To teach us all the procedures we need to know as divers we started off in the morning in a hotel swimming pool which was freezing! We spent the whole morning in it practising exercises like what to do when you run out of air etc. We both manage everything and Murray had no problems in the water. After this we headed off for lunch at Abu Ghazis which was nice and filling and then we headed back to the dive centre. As we started to get our stuff together to get ready for our second open water dive, we saw big black clouds heading our way and as we headed off to our diving location it started to rain, it continued on and off until the end of the trip. Once at the dive spot were made to do a physical test. This involved swimming to a buoy in the water and back to the boat and then treading water for 10mins. We both managed this and I even beat this macho Canadian guy who had made a kerfuffle earlier about something. The swimming out to the buoy was really tough going as the waves were against us and we kept getting water in our mouths and eyes which was not nice. Anyway after this we got our diving kit on and got into the water. Murray was then taken down to see if he could equalize this time, he managed to get a bit deeper but still had a pain in one ear. I then heading down with the instructor and went over the stuff we had done in the pool. By the time I surfaced it was raining heavily so we packed up and headed back.

The End
