Our first day of work and its Saturday! Our new boss, a good friend of Shaun and Jo, Rodger, who owns a ship write called Maintec. Our first job for him in the morning was to clean up his dingy which was also used by the work. This involved getting it out of the water with a small crane; taking all the barnacles and general sea stuff off the bottom and then washing it down; adding some very corrosive stuff which reacts with the barnacle glue to remove it. We then flipped it over cleaned out the inside and made the rubber almost look new. We then fitted the engine back on ,which by the way we did take off at the start, and when for a spin round the mega yachts with first me driving and then Murray. The size of the dingy is a bit bigger than Michael Thompsons small one (thought you would like to know Craibs).

By this time it was lunch time and we had finished are first half day at work and headed off to the local bar where we met a friend of Rodger and Shaun’s called Silly Steven who was a white local trying to be a South African. Then we headed to the Chinese and onto home.
