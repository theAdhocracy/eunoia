So hai there, long time no speak. It’s been quite a few days since _my_ last blog post. Why? Largely laziness in the beginning and then a desire to do something a little different. The problem is I now have my head set with achieving this “different” blog but keep coming up against barriers.

Basically we’re trying to go a little more towards multimedia, with the success of everything else we’ve been branching out into. So I thought it would be fun to have a vlog (or video blog for people who don’t butcher the english language). Only it’s been proving pretty hard to actually set one up.

Me and Mike have just spent over an hour recording ourselves, largely talking utter rubbish and mucking around, but with some serious stuff. Don’t worry, the video blog won’t be an hour long; it will be ten minutes max and hopefully not even that, we were going to edit it down. Unfortunately when I came to edit it I found the audio was missing. Completely. Sigh.

So despite it recording perfectly in all three test runs and recording perfectly after the actual blog was shot, we still have nothing. Except, that is, about 40 seconds of purely visual content from the muted video.
