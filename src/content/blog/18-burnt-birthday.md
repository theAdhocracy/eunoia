For anyone who hasn't yet realised, today was Mike's 19th Birthday so yay for him; I thought about making him do a birthday blog however he decided to watch a Will Ferrell film instead (says it all). We didn't really do anything all that "special" i.e. nothing we wouldn't have done anyway, although Sean suffered Mexican cuisine at a local tex-mex for the occasion; oh and thanks to Jo who made sure there was chocolate cake as well!

However we certainly did something I doubt Mike's done before on a birthday. We met up with Muriel and Gary (who were at J&amp;S's anniversary meal on the night we arrived) who kindly let us travel to Tintamarre, a small island off the coast of St. Maarten with a pretty rich history for what is in reality a large sand bar. No one lives there now although at one point it even had a small airstrip; the soil is apparently rich and great fertiliser; it appears to have been fought over a lot, even in WW2 when U-boats patrolled the area (one is supposedly scuttled not far from the island).

We travelled in style, of course, aboard Gary and Muriel's power boat (small, fast but very nice) and spent most of the day chatting and snorkelling. The snorkelling in particular has left both Mike and I with some nice sunburn, especially on our backs. I for one have a large square of perfectly white skin dead centre on my back which I obviously covered well in sunscreen and varying shades of red and purple over the rest which I obviously didn't cover at all...

We also briefly went ashore which led to some hilarity as M&amp;G own an old terrier called Daisy; a squat, OAP of a dog whose completely deaf. Nevertheless once Gary had carried her onto the boats steps she jumped off quite happily into the sea and with 4-inch long legs struck out boldly for the shore...after being prodded in the right direction. Too tired to swim back, Gary placed her on a body board and towed her, which was even more comical.

Whilst on shore we didn't explore too far, just enough for Gary and Jo to show us how to extract some gritty, yellow clay from the ground on the island which we then covered ourselves in. Me and Mike really just did it to cover ourselves in clay, but it actually did work extremely well as an exfoliant which is what most people use it for.

In the evening we returned to St. Maarten and went out to a Mexican for a special birthday meal for Mike. All very nice, although only Mike can be the judge of whether deep-fried ice cream is really a delicacy or an abomination.
