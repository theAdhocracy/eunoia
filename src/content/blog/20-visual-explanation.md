The new photo album is quite fragmented so I felt it needed some explanation; it begins over a week ago, with Mike's Birthday...day. Not much more to add, however margaritas are not to become a regular occurrence. We then have a few shots of the house plus Mike and I dressed oddly in the garden. These definitely do deserve explanation.

A while back Mike's mum requested some photos of J&amp;S's house which we promised to take. However we then came up with a neat idea of doing a video tour, so to create it we stuck a laptop hooked up with a webcam on my back, bolted said webcam to my head using a system of clamps and elastic and then forced me to run around like a prat after Mike for an hour or so. Hopefully that's a little clearer and we'll have the edited Eunoia Cribs (for anyone who gets that joke) online shortly.

The last few photos lead nicely into the other reason for this article: to fill the void of nothingness left by the weekend. Saturday, largely due to my inability to wake up and compounded by a flat tire, was a no show. We had hoped to visit Loterie Farm (correct spelling I promise) which is set in the last secondary rain forest on the island. It's primary purpose is an eco-venture but really its a glorified assault course with a few hiking trails thrown in for good measure. We had planned to go with Pat &amp; Rodger and take part in some zip-wiring and tree-top assault course stuff but arrived too late and had to settle for Sex in the Trees; the name of the cocktail I ordered, despite what Rodger would say.

Sunday was pretty much just chillaxin, watching some films and generally not doing much at all. The Heineken Regatta was still ongoing so every now and then some more yachts could be seen racing by from the balcony but otherwise we just sat around and waited for the big party that evening to celebrate the closing of said Regatta @ Kimshaw Beach, with Orange Grove and the Wailers (of Bob Marley fame) playing live. In brief it was a good night out; we pretty much missed Orange Grove's set; the guest Jamaican DJ was awful and the Wailers were pretty amazing.

So that's everyone caught up. We are still working on the promised vlog to do with Work as well as the video tour. I'll also try and pester Mike into contributing a little more 😛
