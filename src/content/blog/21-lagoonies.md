Been a while since I did a blog, I think I might be typing that a lot over the next 6 months but at least you will get a few, better than none I’m sure. Anyway as Murray may of said (I don’t read his blogs) we have been working for the past 3 weeks or so and so not much has really gone on apart from at the weekends. However last weekend we had an extended weekend because of the Heineken Regatta (which started on the Friday) so we had the Friday off.

The Heineken Regatta is the biggest sailing event in the Caribbean and about 200 yachts took part this year. It’s a 3 day event and there are parties every night after a day of racing and a big party on the beach on the final day where live bands usually perform. So anyway we did nothing on Friday coz we were really tired from working so so hard as you know we do. On the Saturday we tried and failed to go zip lining n stuff at Lottie farm. And then on Sunday evening we heading off to the beach party where there were The Wailers (the guys who were the backing band for Bob Marley), Orange Grove (a local Reggae band) and some Djs. We caught the end of Orange Grove but they sounded good. We then listened to a few different Djs which was cool and then after a few hours The Wailers came on, they were amazing and played a few of Bob’s hits like “Get up, Stand up”, “Jamming”, “Could you be loved” as well as some of their own. It was all fun and we both had a good time. On the Monday it was back to work on a gunboat called Kanaloa (more about what we did later) where we had started fitting it with new windows. At some point in the afternoon I drop a power cable connector thingy into the lagoon (which apparently costs a lot) So I had to dive down and get it. You would think it would be easy but the lagoon water is a dark green because of all the chemicals and waist in it so I had to borrow a snorkel and dive down about 3 meters. I could not see anything until I reached the bottom but did manage to get it first go. After this I had to borrow one of the guy’s spare pair of shorts and wait for my shorts and boxers to dry.
