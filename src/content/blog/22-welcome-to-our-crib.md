Hopefully most people understand what this vlog is all about, but perhaps some of you are sitting back entirely perplexed. To save further head scratching, we'll try and clear some potential questions up.

First and foremost: the editing. Why "rap" music? Why quick walking sequences? Why strange subtitles? Why "Cribs"? The answer to them all is that we decided to parody (badly) a relatively well known series on MTV called Cribs. The series is all about touring rich/famous peoples houses hence the comparison between that and what we were doing. The music and edits were all done to mock that series so apologies if they just gave you headaches. We found them funny.

The poor quality? No idea, we shot in HD 720p, you can't get higher. However due to files becoming corrupted and Mike's laptop not being able to record edited files in HD we seem to have lost a whole lot of quality. We'll try and not do that next time.

The camera is also quite shakey which doesn't help much. We were shooting using a webcam fastened to my head so everywhere I looked the webcam followed. Unfortunately I have a bad habit of forgetting that I had a webcam on my head and being distracted by bright shiny objects which is why I'm often not looking at Mike when he's talking and we have a good ten seconds of a picture in J&amp;S's bedroom. The webcam also limited audio range and occasionally blurs if the laptop can't keep up. So not the best setup and I looked ridiculous throughout but it was fun so meh.

The carjackers at the end were Sean and Roger. Apologies to Roger who has spent many years avoiding being on the web and is now mentioned frequently in blogs, has photos on facebook and is now on youtube. Still all publicity is good publicity, right? \*Disclaimer\* They were in fact changing the rusted on wheel that had a flat, not stealing the car. Not that they would have gotten very far trying to break in by hammering the wheels...

The audio we used is all royalty free and legal so no worries there.

Um, that about wraps this up. Any questions, any issues, anything at all please email or comment below.
