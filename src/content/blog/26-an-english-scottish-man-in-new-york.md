Getting to JFK airport was fine, apart from us messing up the immigration leaflets that we had to fill in. Apparently their new ESTA system (which we have signed up to) is not in place yet, which is a pain coz it would mean we would not have to worry about these darn leaflets that have the same info on as our passports......ARRGGHHHHHH Anyway from JFK we got on the airports "wonderful" sky rail system (note: the sarcasm) which is really hard to use because they have a crap map key so you can't tell which lines go where. So after riding round the airport for a while we found our way to the underground. Now after using the underground for 2 days it's really quite easy but at that point in time we were tired and didn't read stuff right so it took us about 2 hours and a bit to get to our hostel (Jazz on the City). Our room is very basic with bunk beds and a combined shower and toilet thingy that's very small. As of a few hours ago we have poor internet access too but have found that we get free wi-fi at the top of the hostel (pics coming soon).

Our first full day in New York started off with a cream cheese bagel from a vender which was very nice and filling. We then headed down town following the edge of central park which is very nice. Half way down we spotted America's Natural History Museum and so decided to spend some time there. The original thought it would take a few hour to go round but it took 3 or 4 as it is MASSIVE! And has so many different levels. It was well laid out and the exhibits for the stuffed animals were amazing. We didn't even get to see all of the sections as we had to get some lunch and head downtown. For lunch we had hotdogs which didn't taste much different from the ones back home. Then we carried on following central park down. As we got to the end of central park massive sky scrapers surrounded us and it began to get a lot more crowded as we headed towards Times Square. When we got there it was crazy busy but it looked really cool with all the flashing billboards. After staying there for a while we headed for the Empire State building.

On the way Murray thought that some black lads were handing out free cds of their music so he stopped to get one. Big mistake! as I then got caught up in it, one of them ask me my name and then proceeded to sign a cd for me and ask for a donation telling me that each track was worth $1 and that I should donate if I liked black people. I tried to give mine back saying I didn't have the money but he said "give me a break I've already signed it", did I tell him to sign it? No...... In the end we got away with giving them $5 each which was still a massive pain as I didn't want the darn CD!

Anyway back to walking to the Empire State building. We got there got hassled by tour people at the bottom and then queued up and then went up. It was great at the top and you could even see the Statue of Liberty. After this we also went to the New York public library and Grand central station which were both really nice building and very ornate inside. We then walked all the way back to the hostel which took ages and that was the end of day 1.
