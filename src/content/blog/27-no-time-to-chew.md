Currently sitting outside a Starbucks in sunny Los Angeles wondering why I don't feel hungry despite having eaten nothing for nearly twenty four hours but a complimentary biscuit...

You could say this morning was rushed, but I think that would have been missing the point. This morning was just unnecessary. We arrived at the airport, well, cutting it tight despite being up four hours before hand. We arrived though to find that the flight we were on runs four or five times a day; why therefore we were booked into the earliest, busiest flight with the longest wait at the other end I have no idea. Still we made it, quickly and a whole lot more smoothly than our last few encounters with JFK and waiting ten hours during the day definitely beats nearly 18 hours at night.

In general I enjoyed New York. I find a simple benchmark of how much I enjoyed somewhere is would I return? Would I live there? I think with NY the answer to both would be yes; I have no intention of living there but if I did wind up doing so I think I would enjoy myself. It's a nice city with certainly a whole lot of flavour and spectacle; it's not too expensive, the people are fine from our experience and the whole system actually works really well.

That said, we had run out of things to do, so I am glad we left. We spent most of our last day walking from one place to another without really doing anything. The Guggenheim museum was closed; we never did find anywhere selling nice sunglasses; Madison square gardens was a maze; the Nintendo store was woefully small and lacking in Gashapon and we really just didn't have the energy to do much more exploring.

Well off to New Zealand it is...which should be a whole lot smaller than the US!
