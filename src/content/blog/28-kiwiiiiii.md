As you may have seen from some of Murray's Tweets we are in Auckland, New Zealand!

We got here on Monday morning at around 8 NZ time after a 13 hour flight from LA, California. During the flight we missed a whole day, the 28 march, and will never see it again, however we will make the time back up heading home I think. Waiting in LA for 10 hours was a bit of a drag as there was no free internet. The flight was ok and there were some good movies on so that was fine. Slum dog Millionaire is really good so you should go and see it Jo&amp;Sean!

Getting a bus from Auckland Airport was fine and it was easy to find the hostel. The room we are in is a 10 bed dorm so it's quite crowded as it's a small room. The other people in the room are all really quiet and don't talk much. The only think that sucks about here is the Internet which we have to pay for as we can't find any free ones to connect. There also seems to be a lack of plugs in the room which is a pain. Also we couldn't check into our room on the Monday until 1 which sucked as I felt rubbish from being in transit for 2 days ish. Oh and I also picked up a cold on the flight over which mega sucks!

First day in Auckland was nice, we started off with a $2 breakfast at Denny's and then walked to the Harbour and then a little towards the bridge which isn't anything as cool as Sydney's. After this we picked up lunch from Subway and headed to what we thought was a large park. It turned out to be like a jungle (check pictures). After coming out of the jungle part we went to the Auckland War Memorial Museum which is in the middle of the "park". Its not as big as the New York one but it was still really good as admission was free and we could pay $9 to see Sue the Largest T-rex fossil ever found! So all round a good first day in Auckland. We are only spending 3 days here and then heading further North for 2 days to a place which I can't remember what its called...
