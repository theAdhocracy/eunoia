As most of you reading should know we are now in Australia and have been for a week now. We flew into Sydney and were pick up by the Burgess’, who’s home we would be staying at for a few days. On our first full day in Oz we were shown around Manley which is the area in which the Burgess family live. The next day we headed into Sydney and saw all things which tourists would see like the Sydney Opera house, The Harbour Bridge and the Sky tower (which we went up). After a few more days in Sydney we headed to Newcastle on the train because our bus pass, that we have to go up the coast, does not go through Newcastle. Getting the train was easy enough and we soon arrive there. We then got picked up by the Psaros’s and taken to their home.

For our first day in Newcastle we decided to head down to the beach and jump so waves. Finding our way down to the beach was fine and I got in the water straight away. Murray had second thoughts as the waves we rather large. But he came in and we swam about for a while. It was hard to swim back because the current was so strong and it also moved us down the beach quite quickly. After I got dropped by a wave we got out and walked along to a sea pool, which is a massive outdoor pool with sea water in, for those of you who wanted to know. We liked the look of it so swam there for a while too before grabbing some lunch and heading back. However we found getting back a little harder and got a little lost so it took us 3 hours to get back instead of around 30mins. In the 3 hours we managed to befriend a small dog which followed us for ages (we don’t know if it got back home). And reach an area off the map that had been given to us by Kathy Burgess (Thanks for the map it helped loads). It’s a good job both of us can handle long distance walking!

One night Jim Psaros very kindly took us to see a rugby league match with the Newcastle Knights and the Brisbane Broncos. I normally don’t like rugby but the league stuff was quicker than union so I didn’t mind it. The match was good and the Knight’s ended up winning 28 – 10. Just after the start of the match it started to rain and it quickly became a thunderstorm. At half time the Stadium was struck by lightning and all the lights went off. It was then 50mins before the kick off of the second half. To our amusement all the people watching the match from the grassy area got soaked, but most left as it was so wet however some crazy people stayed! Overall a first good week in Oz, a big thanks to both families for feeding us and washing our cloths etc. We are now back in Sydney and will start our bus trip tomorrow!
