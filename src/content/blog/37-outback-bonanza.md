The dust settles from the last failed attempt. Tempers are beginning to fray; eyes narrow as both hunter and prey pant heavily. The spectators drop silent as the goats bleat and huddle further together. A quick lunge; a grasping hand shoots out and connects with solid, gnarled bone. No escape now - the goat is pulled roughly over to where the other three stand. In comes the lifter to take control, arms reaching over the flanks and gripping the struggling animals’ legs - one in each hand. With a sudden wrench the entire beast is flipped onto its back and secured with a single knee. Quickly the brander returns and places the metal onto the goats flank: 1,2,3 and off – almost there. Now the final teammate comes into play, gripping the horns and once more forcing the struggling animal across the muddy pen. As the brander returns the tool to its position and pulls open the door, the goat is finally thrown into the second pen; a cheer goes up from those watching around the ring. Triumphant the four wipe the sweat from their brows and leave the pen to take their own seats and await their scores.

Returning to the main farm once all the goats have been mustered and the shooting lessons are complete we gather around a great log table and await the full roast meal being prepared on the nine year old fire behind us. Once full we return to the campfire and head off in groups for a lesson in whip-snapping; the sound of leather breaking the sound barrier exploding through the night air once every few minutes.

Finally the blanket is taken from the contraption in the corner and the leather hide of the metal bull comes to life. Each in turn attempts to ride for more than eight seconds; some manage twice that time, others are flung down in half. Never mind, though, as not a single person leaves without a smile on their face.

So ends yet another day in the life of Oz Experience and the Kroombit cattle ranch.
