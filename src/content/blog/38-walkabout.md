It’s been a while since my last blog (as always) and we are now almost at Cairns. Since then we have travelled through Byron Bay, Brisbane, Mooloolaba, Rainbow Beach, 1770, Kroombit, Airlie Beach/Whitsundays and are now in Townsville waiting to get a ferry across to Magnetic Island, where we will be staying for two nights before heading up to Cairns.

Byron Bay is a really nice small town but it is FULL of backpackers, the beach is really good as the sand is really fine, there are some nice waves and the water is a light green turquoise. We stayed in Byron for about 7 days or so and had good accommodation, It was really chilled back and for most of the days we would go to the beach in the morning and watch movies in the afternoon. We did two activities here which were sea kayaking and mountain biking. The Sea kayaking was really good and great fun; we even saw some dolphins at the end. The mountain biking was nice as there was just 3 of us but it was expensive for the amount of time we got to bike.

After Byron we went to Brisbane which was a bit of a shock going from a small town to a massive city. Brisbane is a really nice city but there’s not much to do there. We went up the clock tower and saw the man made lagoon with real sand and the “Brisbane eye” which is just a not as good version of the London eye. From Brisbane we had a day trip to the lovely Morton Island which is the third largest sand island in the world. We had an amazing day there as the weather was perfect and we went snorkelling, which was ok, and did some sand boarding which was wicked fun. Although for some reason girls always go further than the boys. We both try really hard and could never beat them. We don’t have any pictures of sand boarding because of the amount of really fine sand. Oh and we got half price sausage rolls on the way back on the ferry, score!

Mooloolaba was our first overnight stop after Brisvagas, Mooloolaba is an alright small coastal town, but there was not much to do there and the beach was not very good compared to Byron Bay. The waves were none existent and the water a bit murky. On leaving Mooloolaba it started to rain and only stopped just before we got to Rainbow Beach. Rainbow Beach is a tiny place with only a handful of shops and stuff, this is mainly because people only go there to get to Frazer Island, which is the largest sand island in the world. We didn’t go there because of how expensive it is. I’m also quite happy we didn’t because for the next three days it rained! And wet sand is no fun.

Next it was 1770, well a town just below it. 1770 got its name because it’s the year that Captain Cook landed there and so they decided to call it that for some strange reason. It continued to rain all the way to 1770 and while we were there. We stayed in a nice hostel which was kinda tropical and we saw a grey kangaroo and a wallaby.

Kroombit was after 1770 and was quite good fun but would of been better if we had know more people. The best bit was firing a 12 gauge shotgun for more on Kroombit read Murrays blog.

We had clear sky all the way to Airlie Beach and throughout our stay there. Airlie is another small town and is very expensive and there is not much to do there because there’s no beach. However there is a man made lagoon which I remember from visiting 9 years ago. The main activity to do from Airlie is to go to the Whitsundays Island. We went there on a day drip and did some snorkelling on the way which was really good and the coral was vibrant and you could see a good few feet. There were many different fish from the Caribbean and a few large Angel fish. Whitsundays Island has pure silica sands so it’s very white and fine. Every year NASA come over and takes some to use on their space shuttles because it’s so good. I really liked it there as it reminded me of Sanna Bay which was nice.
