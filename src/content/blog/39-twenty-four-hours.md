It started on the 25th in Oz; I woke up to a cloudy day on Magnetic Island only to find out that my favourite football team Newcastle United had been relegated for the English Premiership. It’s the first time in 16 years that they have gone back done and I still can’t believe it. Not even the great Alan Shearer could save us; at least we might win some games now. Anyway after this news we went out for a walk round Magnetic Island. This was cut short when I cut my foot open on a stick poking out the sand on a beach. It then started to rain and didn’t stop until the next morning. It the next morning when I received a text from my dad telling me grandpa Craib had passed away. I knew he had been having his up and downs but it still came as a shock and I always expected him to always be around. I’m also lucky enough to of had all my grandparents still alive until this point so it will be strange not seeing him ever again. At least I know he has gone to a better place.

He was known as William to some, Bill to other but to me he was grandpa Craib, he was a kind man and very generous. I’ll always remember fishing with him and my dad out on Kilmacomb lock and how my dad came so close to catching a fish. I remember how me and my sister shone would collect nerstursums seeds for him to dry out and later plant. I remember playing for hours up in Granny and Grandpa Craib’s loft with toy cars while he sat there fixing/making fly fishing fish hooks. I remember how he would always tell me off for slurping my soup and my table manners in general; how he always gave us a chunk of dairy milk chocolate after an evening meal; how he always gave us fizzy drinks and peanuts and I will especially never forget been given a grandpa bag after every time we finished visiting him. I loved him very much and will miss him dearly.

I know this should not really be in a blog but I thought I would write this about him as a kinda tribute thing as I will miss the funeral.
