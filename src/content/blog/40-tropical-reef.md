It was another early start to get picked up by a bus to be taken up to Cape Tribulation, the oldest Rainforest in the World. It also meets the Great Barrier Reef which makes that area the only place in the world where you can find two world heritage sites that meet. On the way up we stopped at an animal habitat place which had lots of different Australian animals, many of which we had already seen but it was still nice to look around. Other stops on the way included lookout points; a tropical fruit picking place that was expensive; an ice cream place that was expensive and a lunch stop. At the ice-cream place we bought a small pot of ice-cream which had some weird flavours like: black soyte, wattle seed, apricot and macadam nuts. I didn’t really like it and would rather of had a soft cone ice-cream from McDonald’s for 50c (yes that 25p) than pay $5 for what we got. Eventually we got to our destination which was a lovely hostel right on the beach, which had nice cabins and lots of big golden orb spiders around. Not only was it nice but I got FREE bacon and scrambled eggs for 2 breakfasts, it was amazing!

The next morning we got picked up by a bus and taken to a catamaran called the Rum Runner which would take us out to the Great Barrier Reef. It was a nice cat but not as nice as Kanaloa (the one we fixed in the Caribbean). It took us about 50mins to reach the reef and the weather was not looking very good and we had already sailed through some rain. I decided not to dive because of the weather and because I couldn’t we bothered to waste time getting ready etc and the reef is not deep so I should see as much as a diver. My first snorkel was cool as I saw 1 sea turtle and a ray. The reef is really spectacular and colourful and is the best coral I have ever seen. There were loads of different fish too and some quite big ones. Visibility was also really good and improved as the sun came out in the afternoon. Lunch was nice and afterwards Murray went snorkelling with me as he did a dive in the morning. This time we saw 2 sea turtles, 2 blue spotted rays and a white tip reef shark that was 2m in length. Overall it was a good day and I enjoyed it immensely.

The morning after we woke to find out that a mouse of some kind had bitten through Murray’s day sack to get at some sweets which it must not have liked as it did not take any. I also found a tarantula like spider in one of the men’s showers which I proceeded to poke with my foot which only had a flip flop on. It suddenly jumped onto my foot and bit me giving me the shock of my life. I immediately kicked it off only to see that it had made a tiny little cut on one of my toes. Later on in the day we got picked up and taken back to Cairns, where we shall be for another 4 days until we fly to Singapore.
