We arrived in Singapore, in the evening, and as soon as we stepped out of the airport we were hit by the humidity. I knew from that point on it would be 3 showers a day! We got a taxi to our hostel which took a while to get to. Our hostel, The Mitraa, has amazing air con in the rooms which is really nice after a day in the heat, it also has free internet which we have been making the most of. Oh and the showers are weird as they are basically toilets with a shower above them.

For our first full day in Singapore we decided to walk to Chinatown. On the way we went through Little India, where we were hit by all sorts of different kinds of smells from burning incense to the sweet smelling durian fruit as well as some odd smells from wheelie bins. Down both sides of the streets there were places to eat and stalls selling lots of different things. We found some nice air conditioned malls in Little India which had some massive electronic stores with every electrical item under the sun. After going through Little India we came to a nice park area which took us close to Chinatown. Once there we had lunch at one of the many places to eat. I had Singapore noodles which were really good and cheap too. After lunch we did some sort of Chinese garden walk which was kinda cool. We then found a really cool temple (the massive red one in the pics) and went inside and up to its roof garden which was nice.

On the second day we decided to go to the zoo as we could get a pass that would let us into 3 places, Singapore Zoo, Night Safari and Singapore Bird Park. The Zoo was really packed as we went on a Saturday but we didn’t really have a choice. We got there on a combination of MRT (The Subway) and bus. The MRT seems to always we busy and it was crazy during the weekend. There is always one person on there playing on a Sony PSP (hand held games system) and everyone else is listening to ipods or on the phone. The MRT is very easy to use its quick and very modern and if London is looking to improve it underground it should use Singapore’s as an example. The bus was also easy to get and its all quite cheap. The zoo its self was really good although we found it a pain trying to see all the animals as you have to double back on yourself a lot. After the Zoo we went to the night safari which was kina cool apart from the fact that you couldn’t really see the animals because it was so dark. It was again also really crowded which didn’t help. For a full review of both please read Murrays blog lol.

Day 3 and we decided to go to an island called Sentosa, just off Singapore which has been made into a theme park kina thingy. We got across there on a mono rail thing but you can also get there by gondola which is a lot more expensive. Our first impression was good as the area we were in first was quite nice. After this it went downhill. First off you have to pay to go on or do anything; secondly food and drink is expensive but that was to be expected; thirdly you can see loads of massive oil tanks and oil refiners from the beach. I found this hilarious, who had the idea of building a massive theme park resort thingy a few miles from oil refiners…..nuts. If you ever go to Singapore in the future don’t go to Sentosa unless you have small kids that really want to.

For our final day in we decided to find the Raffles hotel as my mum said it was famous for making a cocktail called the Singapore Sling. I’m so very glad we did go to see it as we found the main business area of Singapore which is very cool as you can see from the pictures. We even saw the “Singapore Eye” and got our picture of the Raffles hotel. We didn't get the famous Sling cocktail as it cost’s $27! There was also the Raffles shopping mall which we went into, it was crazy cool as it had loads of shop you would see in the UK like M&S and Topman. We had lunch in there which was nice and had a McDonald’s 70c ice cream after. In the afternoon we went to the Jurong Bird Park which was kinda crap as you can’t really see many of the birds. I imagine you do however if your there when they feed them. Drinks there were also outrageously expensive with 200ml water costing $3 and 350ml coke for $3.50!

Tomorrow we are off to Kuala Lumpa on the night train which we are getting at 7.40am and so will be travelling in the day. I think that’s funny…..
