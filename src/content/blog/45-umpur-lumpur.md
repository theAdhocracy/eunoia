If you read the last blog about our fun train adventure you will know that we did eventually get to Kuala Lumpur. From the train station we got a taxi to our hostel which was much cheaper, thanks to Kuala Lumpur using ringgits. Once at the hostel we were told to take out shoes off before going in, as it is Malay custom to do so when entering anyone’s house. Our room was had four beds but did not have air con which sucked a lot. Other than that it was very nice and we got free breakfast. I will now always check if they have air con before booking next time as the heat gets unbearable. I would much rather live with Cumbria’s wet weather, than the sticky humidity you get here! However Murray seams to cope with it ok and he hates air con, because apparently it dries his eyes out.

That night we went out and had a look around the area we were staying in. There were lots of shopping malls nearby and we had a massive mall right in front of our hostel called Times Square. You could get all sorts of things there and after a few days we just got our food from there all the time as it was right at our front door, had a top floor food court and many other places to eat. So we spent a lot of our time in their riding escalators up and down, up and down. We could also get on the mono rail through the mall which helped. Oh and it had air con throughout!

Anyway enough about escalators, for our first full day in Kuala Lumpur we decided to go see the KL Tower, The Petronas Twin Towers and a crafts centre! So we headed off in the heat of the day and walked to each one. The view from the top of the KL Tower wasn’t brilliant as there was smog but we got into a reptile place for free with our Tower ticket which was cool. After the tower we headed to the Twin Towers and once we got there we were stunned by how amazingly cool they were and so took a tone of photos of them from every possible angle. They are by far the best piece of architecture we have seen this whole trip! We then came across an aquarium which was in a conference centre building for some reason. So we had lunch there and went in and saw lots of fish, sharks, turtles and spiders….spiders? Yes for some reason they had a thing on the Amazon so there were frogs and stuff there too as well as massive arapaima! After a long time spent in the aquarium, due to Murray getting carried away with taking pictures, we headed to the craft centre place which was the first place I remembered from the last time I was in Kuala Lumper, 9 years ago. However there was little or nothing going on there so we heading back to the hostel and on the way back I saw a hotel that I knew I’d seen before and that’s because it was the one where me and my family stayed at all those years ago.

Day 2 was another day of checking out cool buildings and stuff so we caught the mono rail, which I heard later was only just being built last time I was here, to somewhere and started working our way down an area where there were lots of buildings of interest. On the way we saw some nice high rise buildings and cool mosques. After going through china town and being offered many DVDs and sunglasses we got to KL’s international museum which I had been to before. We got round it very quickly as it was small and didn’t have much. We then proceeded towards the bird park, but decided we had walked too much and so went home. We also never went to the bird park here after seeing too many birds at the last one we went to in Singapore.

The next day we only had a small section of KL to do as we had seen so much the other day. So we caught the mono rail up to the sculpture garden, took some photos and then decided to get on the hop-on-hop off bus that goes around KL which we had not bothered to use as we like walking places, even if it was crazy humid. So we got on and though it was very nice as it had ice cold air con. However we only cooled down a little after finding out it would cost us 40 ringgits each to use it, so we got off at the next stop and walked back/caught the cheap mono rail, home. In hindsight it’s a good deal if you want to see KL. However we had already and just wanted a cool ride back to the hostel. That night we found out that we would have to move to another hostel the next day as we had forgot to book more nights and for some crazy reason hostels in Malaysia get busy at the weekends, which makes no sense to me…as there’s only ever foreigners in the hostels. Anyway we booked one literally 30 seconds up from our current hostel for 2 nights.
On our 4th day in Kuala Lumpur at 10 in the morning we headed to our new hostel which I thought was amazing! It had air con, nice beds, FREE internet and FREE incredible tasty breakfast! In comparison to our last hostel we had to pay 15 ringgits each for wireless internet and our free breakfast consisted of toast and jam. The free breakfast at the new hostel started with fresh fruit (watermelon and orange slices); toast with butter and honey and finally a roti with curry source. It was amazing. After the great food, which Murray missed as he is lazy and does not get out of bed in time, we headed off to catch a bus to Batu Caves which I remembered well from the last time I went. The only difference this time was that they now have a whacking big golden Hindu statue in front of it and it’s a little more developed. The monkeys where still there too and were funny to watch. The caves themselves were cool and it was an ok view from the top of the 250 stairs.

That was pretty much everything we did in Kuala Lumpur apart from ride the escalators quite a few more times; watched the new terminator in a huge IMAX cinema; get out hair cut; had our feet nibbled by lots of fish at a fish spar thing and tried to post postcards on a Sunday….again. I blame this on the fact that we just lose track of what day it is and generally not thinking. Oh and we went back to our first hostel in Kuala Lumpur for some reason I still do not know, other than we booked our last night there before we left it and could not be bothered to un book it. Yes it was hot and I didn’t sleep well and had jam on toast in the morning… we are so lazy…
