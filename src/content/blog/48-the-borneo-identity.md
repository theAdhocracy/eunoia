This is the one and only time I will use a suggested corny blog title that I did not come up with and blog on request. Anyway if you had not guessed by the title already this blog will be about my first time in Borneo which we flew to from Kuala Lumpur.

Our flight to Borneo was fine, apart from the lack of screens in the back of the chairs which we had had in every plane up until then, but to be fair it was only costing us £50 each to get there. We got a taxi from the airport to our hostel which was situated near the centre of Kota Kinabulu. The taxi ride was cheap compared to the one we got in Singapore which was nice. The hostel we stayed in was ok apart from that we were in a 10 man dorm and the air con only came on after 8pm. Oh and it didn’t have many plugs and there were some funny smells sometimes. That night we had a bit of a wander and went past the markets near the water front which smell horrible because for some reason the locals like to dry out anything they pull out the sea including sea slugs. I found that the streets also smelt a bit funkier than Singapore and KL but I got used to it.

Kota Kinabulu is a nice city and had lots of shopping malls like most East Asian cities we have been to. The market place is a bit of a squeeze not because of the number of people but because it’s set up in a grid and is very narrow and has objects hanging at head banging height, which was nice for me. It sells lots of different souvenirs from nice carvings and purses to blow darts and stuffed frogs. There is also a fruit market but they mainly seemed to have one kind of fruit that looked like a mango but was not. One of the big things that they sell in KK is fake DVD’s and there are loads of shops in the shopping malls selling them as well as people on the streets, who constantly shout “Wana buy DVD?” at you as you walk by them which gets very annoying. The DVDs can range from filmed from the back of a cinema to some really good quality stuff. So it is hit and miss on finding good ones and even if they are bad it’s still only cost you a few quid. It’s also quite funny to see the police outside of the DVD selling shops, as they don’t care.

Food in KK is really good just like KL and Singapore. We had breakfasts at the hostel which consisted of toast and jam as per usual. We had lunch the most at a place where Murray had eaten a lot previously when he came to KK. We would order Roti Canai which is really hard to explain what it is but its most like peshwari naan bread you get with curries at an Indian restaurant. It comes with a chilli source that you dip it in, it tastes really good and I would often order two as they were dirt cheap. Murray once ordered a Roti Tisu which is a Roti with sugar water. It came in a massive cone shape and was also nice. We had most of our evening meals down on the water front where there were lots of nice restaurants which served lots of different Asian dishes; they were always very good and cheap.

As there was not a lot to do in KK we decided to travel to the Kinabulu National Park which has Mt Kinabulu situated in it. We also went to Pouring where they have hot springs. The whole trip took 3 days in total. We caught a mini bus from KK to the National Park. It took 3 hours to get there and I will not forget how hellish those 3 hours were! This was because the mini bus we were in was full; had no air con; was very cramped and we had our day sacks on top of us. I suffered more than Murray due to my long legs and dislike of being in a cramped vehicle. I was also a bit worried about where we were going to sleep that night as you can’t book anything over the internet for where we went. Luckily we found the place Murray had stayed last time which was kinda cool and had great views, but had a slanted toilet block that looked like it could go at any second and billions of moths all of different size, colour and shape. The room also smelt musky and the shower was ice cold and was little more than a dribble. Mt Kinabulu was very cool and massive, it would have been nice to climb it but we did not have the time and it’s also expensive.

The next day we had “fun” trying to get a bus to Pouring. This involved us flagging down buses that went by. However I didn’t want a full bus again and all the buses going past were full so I didn’t want to get on them. Anyway after a while a mini bus came along and the driver said he would take us both to Pouring for 70 Ringgits. So we went with him and it was quite nice as we had the bus to ourselves and had air con! Once there our driver also found us a place to stay which was also cheap and kinda of like a home stay place. The room had just a double bed in it and a fan so it kept us quite cool. The toilet/shower was horrible as it was a squatting toilet and it was very very small. It also smelt really really bad. After getting our room we went looking for bats which Murray saw. He also fell over trying to take a photo which was very funny; we went for a swim after in one of the pools they had in the area. That night there was a massive thunder storm and it rained for ages, we also had to skip dinner as we did not have enough cash on us and there were no ATMs.

The next morning we also skipped breakfast and went on the canopy walk near the hot springs which was really rubbish as there was nothing to see, it was also very short and there was loads of stairs to walk up and down. After the “fantastic” canopy walk we packed our bags and head for home. This time we caught a mini bus to Ranau where we found an ATM and had a really nice man who work for the tourist information centre come up to us and help us get a cheap taxi/mini bus home which was still quite cramped but it did have air con and was much nicer. From Ranau it took us 4 hours to get home and once back we had a massive meal at KFC because we had missed two meals. Our last few days in KK were filled with watching DVDs of the fake variety; eating lots of Roti and walking around a nice Sunday market which had lots of puppies, fish and hamsters. I wanted gerbils quite bad after seeing them as they were so cute! Oh yeah and we went to one of the island off KK. Where we couldn’t snorkel due to little stingy jelly fish and where we also got burnt on our backs. Good times!
