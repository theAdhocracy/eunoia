As we left Singapore for the last time I think we were both pretty tired, worn out from the constant jumping from here-to-there and back again; for that reason alone the South African leg was a godsend. The ability to simply relax for a few weeks with a consistent home base was fantastic and really helped recharge the batteries; being able to use some of suitcases for our road-trip was a similar blessing - I cannot be rid of that backpack soon enough!

Similarly being able to travel around, exploring a country I love, without having to worry about time tables and train routes was fantastic (even if it did mean one or two early rises - not my forte I’m afraid) and just generally kicking back was superb.

I’m an animal nut so the game parks and various animal centres we visited suited me down to the ground; I hope Mike can one day forgive me for dragging him around Monkey Land not once but twice…in a single day!

Watching lions at a kill (even if it was a “man made” one) and seeing my first spots of Aardwolf, Bat-eared Fox and Gemsbok in the wild were brilliant and exactly why I enjoy the SANParks so very much.
Plett similarly appealed for both its stunning beauty (an attribute shared with much of the roadtrip) and the aforementioned wildlife centres; stroking Cheetah and Caracal was certainly an experience, although perhaps not as much as riding an Ostrich! Possibly the weirdest thing we did on the whole trip? I think so.

But of course for me whether it was scanning curios in Cape Town, phoning the UK for directions at Cape Point, watching game or dining on delicious calamari the real enjoyment came from just having my grandparents around. So once more, thanks to both of you it was a really great few weeks and a really great note on which to end the whole world tour!
