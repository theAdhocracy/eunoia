First off I can’t believe Murray stole my last blog name which I had been planning to use since Borneo! Anyway I’m still going to use it as mine will be much better!

Well it’s been one crazy adventure and I’ve had loads of fun and I’m sure Murray has too. I’m now going to tell you about what I thought were the best and worst bits, and generally my overall feeling about each place we went to. Oh and where my favourite place in the world is.

So we started off on a very cold and snowy day and flew from the UK to New York and then onto the Caribbean. I found the Caribbean great fun and a good laugh and very much enjoyed the company of Jo and Shaun. Working for Pat and Rodger at Maintec was also great as we got to blast around in dingy and fit windows in a 4 million dollar gun boat! And because of working there we can now get jobs at a shipwright anywhere in the world because of our amazing gel coating and fibreglass coating skills! Food was crazy good in the Caribbean too as we had Shaun cooking most of the time and the rest was all you can eat barbeque ribs. The very best bits were definitely learning to dive and working for Maintec. The worst part of the Caribbean was that It could get too hot and the nasty mosquitoes which have covered my feet with scares.

After the Caribbean it was New York, which really is spectacular and amazing to walk through even if you destroy your legs doing it like we did. New York food is also very nice and I especially loved the Jewish delis. The best park of New York was standing in Times Square both at day and night and going up the Rockefellers building. The worst part was probable the subways as they were a little dodgy but nothing bad happened to us.

Then from New York we flew to LA and then onto New Zealand. New Zealand is the one of the most scenic places in the world and has everything from mountains caped in snow to golden sandy beaches. It really is jaw dropping sometimes and it’s also incredible because of its size. The people we meet on the kiwi bus were such a laugh and I really don’t think it would have been as good without them. Food was ok but we were mainly on budget stuff like noodles etc. The best bits in New Zealand for me were white water rafting, luging and of course bungee jumping which is just phenomenal. The worst bits, well there wasn’t any that I can remember!

Next we were down under in Australia where people like to call us poms. We started off in Sydney and then heading up the east coast to Cairns. We really enjoyed having home cooking again in Sydney with the Burgess family and had a nice time in Newcastle with the Psaros lot. Food was kind of similar to NZ but we had more BBQ stuff. The very best bits of Oz where being up in Daintree Rainforest and snorkelling on the Great Barrier Reef and body boarding at Byron Bay. The Worst part was the fact that the Oz bus was not as good as the kiwi one.
After Oz we were in Singapore which is a pretty cool city and fairly massive too. Here it was really nice walking through the streets with all the burning incense and all the different shops and stalls. The only problem is the humidity which is crazy. Food in Singapore was really good as you would expect with all the noodles and rice and stuff. The best bit in Singapore was the architecture which is very photographic as you may have seen, also some of the temples and china town where good too. The worst part was of course the humidity and getting the train to Kuala Lumpur, oh and that rubbish tourist island just off Singapore called Sentosa. On another note, I love air con.

Kuala Lumpur was next and it too like Singapore has some very nice architecture, we especially loved the Petronas Towers and so took loads of pictures of them at day and night. The food in KL is amazing and better than Singapore so meal times where always nice as long as you didn’t get any bones in the food. The best part of KL was most of it really as it has lots of great places to visit and I can’t think of any worst parts apart from the humidity.

Our second last place to visit was Borneo which was really nice apart from the humidity again, it really got to me here because for half of our time there we were in a room at night with just a fan so I didn’t get much sleep. There’s not really much to see in Kota Kinabalu but the market place was kind of cool and creepy. The food was AWESOME! I wish they made rotis in the UK. The best bits were going to see Mt. Kinabalu, Pouring hot springs and going to one of the islands of KK. The worst part of Borneo was people shouting “DVD” at us all the time and the journey to and from Kinabalu National Park in a cramped minivan.

Finally our last stop was South Africa, at this point I was ready to go home but I’m glad we didn’t as South Africa was really good. Like I said in my blog on SA it was nothing like I thought it would be and in my book it was the second best place for scenery out of all the places we went to. It was very nice being looked after by Murrays Grandparents and we stayed in so many nice places and the accommodation was always 10 times better than any hostel we had stayed in (apart from the scary rangers house in the Zebra Park) The food was very good too and I enjoyed eating the animals I saw in the parks, yum. The best bit of SA was definitely going to the game parks as I’ve never done anything like it before and it was great seeing lions in the wild up close. The worst part for me was getting the train into Cape Town after being told muggings were very common and maybe seeing too many animals lol.

So yeah that’s that it in a nut shell really and I’ll never forget it, especially as I have so many photos from it. I thought you might like to find out which place I liked the best for food, people, city etc so I’ve done some rankings below.

Best Food:

1. Kuala Lumpur
2. Borneo
3. Caribbean

Best locals:

1. Singapore
2. Kuala Lumpur
3. Caribbean

Best City:

1. New York
2. Kuala Lumpur
3. Sydney

Best Scenery:

1. New Zealand
2. South Africa
3. Borneo

Best Transport:

1. Singapore
2. Kuala Lumpur
3. New York

So yeah that’s about it from this final blog from me. But I would like to say a big thank you to everyone we stayed will along the way for feeding us and washing our cloths and saving us a few quid. A big thank you to Family who sent me money for my Christmas and birthday that went towards the trip. And a massive thank you to anyone who prayed for us as we never came to any harm or had any big problems. And finally thanks Murray for good times that I’ll not forget!

Anyway thanks for following us if you have been reading this blog. I never thought I’d like doing it but it’s been fun and kept the parents happy. Unfortunately we will not be continuing this blog about us but we will maybe start blogs about graphic design and coding if University is not too stressful. Oh and by the way my favourite place in the world is still Sanna Bay, you just can’t beat it…

See ya,

Mike
